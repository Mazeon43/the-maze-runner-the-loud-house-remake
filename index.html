Welcome to The Maze Runner: The Loud House Remake fanfiction website!

Written by Mazeon, this project is a retelling of The Maze Runner featuring characters from The Loud House. While following the general premise of the original films, the story also introduces original characters, locations, storylines, and changes to existing events. The goal is to create my own version of the series while expanding on its world and characters.

This story is intended for mature audiences, as it contains strong language, graphic violence, disturbing content, and other themes that may not be suitable for younger readers.

The project is currently planned as a four-part series, with the possibility of additional spin-off stories released between installments.

This website serves as a place to read the stories and explore additional information about the characters, factions, locations, and lore. Thanks for visiting, and I hope you enjoy the project!
